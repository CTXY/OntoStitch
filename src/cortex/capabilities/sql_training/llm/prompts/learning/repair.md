# Repair

Resolve supplied fixed Diagnosis targets and Assessment feedback while preserving correct behavior. Assessment-only repair may have no Diagnosis target. Do not repeat Diagnosis, re-extract the whole case or expand fixed targets.

Locate the affected knowledge and make a coherent correction to any semantic type or edge; a relation defect does not automatically require a Rule. Use confirmed error signals only where observed. Preserve valid bindings, complete formulas and necessary relationships; revert unsupported tentative edits.

Consult the applicable guide/registry when needed. Do not invent roles, dependencies or policies just to make SQL replay pass. Once the supported correction is known, edit and hand off.
